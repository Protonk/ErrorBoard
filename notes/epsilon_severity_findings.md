with severity as the dependent variable. Per pair where the model errs,
we compute ULP-distance and absolute log-damage.

Stratify by result mantissa m_c (normal-result only). Hypothesis: damage
per bin tracks ε(m_c).

## Interpretation

If damage at result-mantissa m_c tracks ε(m_c), the model's errors are
concentrated *in real magnitude* where the formal residual peaks. If
instead damage is constant (always 1 ULP), the model is making
rounding-class mistakes regardless of m_c. If max ULP error is mostly 1,
ε's shape might only surface in a different stratification.
